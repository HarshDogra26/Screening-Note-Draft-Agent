title: Meridian Petrochemicals reports full-rate operation across Jurong crackers
---

Meridian Petrochemicals confirmed that both of its Jurong Island steam crackers operated
at full rates through the fourth quarter.

The company operates two crackers on the site. Jurong Cracker 1, commissioned in 2012,
runs on mixed feed. Jurong Cracker 2, commissioned in 2021, is naphtha-based. Meridian
states that its combined ethylene capacity across the two units is 1,450 kta, which the
company describes as the largest single-operator ethylene position in Southeast Asia.

The Jurong complex also includes HDPE, LDPE and polypropylene lines and a propylene
recovery unit.

Meridian noted that it is not related to Gulf Meridian Chemicals of Saudi Arabia, with
which it is occasionally confused in trade press; the two companies have no common
ownership.
