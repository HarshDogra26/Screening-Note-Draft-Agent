title: Luzon Resins reports operational summary for the year
---

Luzon Resins Corporation reported stable operations across its Batangas complex for the
year, with no unplanned outages at the cracker.

The company said its polypropylene line ran at high rates through the period. Capacity
figures were not restated in this summary.
