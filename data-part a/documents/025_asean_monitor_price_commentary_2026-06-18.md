title: Southeast Asia olefins price commentary - first half review
---

Southeast Asian ethylene spot assessments were volatile through the first half of 2026.
The Monitor attributes the April peak principally to concurrent supply outages: a planned
turnaround at Pengerang, which the operator extended into a force majeure declaration,
overlapping with the tail of the Cilegon furnace replacement programme in March.
Assessments eased through May and June as both units returned.

Feedstock costs contributed. Asian naphtha firmed steadily across the period on Northeast
Asian refinery maintenance, compressing cracker margins even where olefin prices rose.

Readers should note that the Monitor's propylene assessment for Southeast Asia moved from
a spot to a contract basis with effect from January 2026, following a change in how
regional volumes are transacted. The resulting step in the published series reflects the
change of basis and should not be read as a market movement. The Monitor advises against
comparing pre- and post-January propylene values directly.

**The Monitor does not publish price forecasts or outlooks.** This commentary is a review
of assessed movements only. Enquiries about forward expectations cannot be answered from
Monitor publications.
