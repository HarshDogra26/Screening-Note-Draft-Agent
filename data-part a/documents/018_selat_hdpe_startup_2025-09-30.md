title: Selat Kimia starts up second Pengerang polyethylene line
---

Selat Kimia Berhad has started up a 380 kta HDPE line at its Pengerang complex. The line
uses slurry loop technology and takes ethylene from the existing Pengerang cracker.

The company said the addition brings the Pengerang complex to two polyethylene lines,
alongside the existing 340 kta LLDPE unit.
