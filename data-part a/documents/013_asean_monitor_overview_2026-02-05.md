title: Southeast Asia olefins - structural overview
---

Southeast Asian olefins capacity remains concentrated in a small number of integrated
complexes, with Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand accounting for the large
majority of regional ethylene output.

Naphtha remains the dominant feedstock across the region, with the exception of Brunei,
where ethane is used. Propane dehydrogenation has grown as a source of on-purpose
propylene, with units in operation in Malaysia and under construction in the Philippines.

The Monitor does not publish aggregate regional capacity totals in this bulletin.
Readers requiring totals should consult the Monitor's capacity database, which is
updated quarterly and lists units individually.
