title: Bengal Petrochem progresses Haldia PDH project
---

Bengal Petrochem Ltd said construction of its 500 kta propane dehydrogenation unit at
Haldia, India, remains on schedule for startup in 2027.

The unit will supply on-purpose propylene to the company's existing downstream units.
Bengal Petrochem also operates a naphtha cracker and an HDPE line at the site.
