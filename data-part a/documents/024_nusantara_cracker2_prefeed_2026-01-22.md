title: Nusantara Olefins begins pre-FEED for second Cilegon cracker
---

Nusantara Olefins has begun pre-front-end engineering design work for a potential second
steam cracker at Cilegon. The company said no investment decision has been taken and no
capacity, feedstock configuration or schedule has been fixed.

The company said a decision would not be expected before 2028.
