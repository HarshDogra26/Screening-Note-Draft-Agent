title: Andaman Polymer Group provides update on Map Ta Phut PP Line 3
---

Andaman Polymer Group today provided an update on the construction schedule for
Polypropylene Line 3 at Map Ta Phut.

The company said mechanical completion has moved from the second quarter of 2028 to the
fourth quarter of 2028. Two factors were cited. First, the environmental permit
amendment covering the revised flare tie-in took longer than the company had assumed in
its original schedule. Second, the appointed civil contractor requested a schedule
extension after encountering unsuitable ground conditions at the foundation stage,
which required additional piling.

Andaman said the capital estimate is unchanged and that the delay does not affect the
existing two polypropylene lines or the cracker.
