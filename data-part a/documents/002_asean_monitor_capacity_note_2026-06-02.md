title: Regional capacity note - Indonesia olefins
---

The Monitor's June capacity review lists Cilegon Cracker 1 (Nusantara Olefins) at a
confirmed nameplate ethylene capacity of 1,050 kta.

The Monitor notes that the operator has publicly referenced a higher figure following
the March 2026 furnace replacement programme. The Monitor's position is that the
additional volume is contingent on a debottlenecking step that has not yet been
independently verified, and it will continue to carry the lower figure until operating
data supports a revision.

Readers tracking Indonesian ethylene balances should be aware that published figures
for this unit currently differ depending on source. The Monitor does not publish
estimates for units where the operator has not disclosed nameplate capacity.
