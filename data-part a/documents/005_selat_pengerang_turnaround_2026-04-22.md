title: Selat Kimia completes Pengerang turnaround
---

Selat Kimia Berhad has completed a scheduled turnaround at its Pengerang cracker. The
unit was offline for 38 days. All downstream units, including the propane dehydrogenation
unit and the polyethylene and polypropylene lines, have returned to normal rates.

The company reported no change to nameplate capacities as a result of the turnaround.
Selat Kimia said the next major turnaround at Pengerang is scheduled for 2030.
