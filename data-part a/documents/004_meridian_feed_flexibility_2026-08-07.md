title: Meridian to add feedstock flexibility at Jurong Cracker 1
---

Meridian Petrochemicals will modify the feed system at Jurong Cracker 1 to allow a
higher proportion of LPG in the cracker diet. Engineering work is scheduled to begin in
the first quarter of 2027 and the company expects to complete the tie-ins during a
planned turnaround.

The company said the modification is intended to improve margin resilience when naphtha
cracking economics deteriorate, and that it does not change the nameplate ethylene
capacity of the unit.

No capital figure was disclosed. The company said the work does not affect Jurong
Cracker 2.
