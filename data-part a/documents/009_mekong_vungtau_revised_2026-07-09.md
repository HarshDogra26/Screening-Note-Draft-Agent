title: Mekong Chemical Industries revises Vung Tau startup to 2028
---

Mekong Chemical Industries has revised the expected startup of its Vung Tau steam
cracker from 2027 to 2028.

The company attributed the revision to delayed delivery of the cracked gas compressor
and to slower-than-planned progress on the site's marine offloading facility. Mekong
said the ethylene design capacity of 750 kta is unchanged, as is the 300 kta HDPE line.

This announcement supersedes the schedule guidance issued by the company in January 2026.
