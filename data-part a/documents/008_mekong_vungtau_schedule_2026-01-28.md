title: Mekong Chemical Industries confirms Vung Tau cracker schedule
---

Mekong Chemical Industries confirmed that construction of its Vung Tau steam cracker is
progressing and that the company is targeting startup in 2027.

The cracker is designed for 750 kta of ethylene on naphtha feed. An associated HDPE line
of 300 kta is being built alongside it. The company said engineering is complete and
that major equipment deliveries began in the fourth quarter of 2025.
