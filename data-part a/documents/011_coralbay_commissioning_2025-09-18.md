title: Coral Bay Olefins commissions Sungai Liang complex
---

Coral Bay Olefins has commissioned its Sungai Liang ethane cracker and associated HDPE
line in Brunei. The cracker uses ethane feedstock supplied under a long-term agreement.

The company said it does not disclose nameplate capacity for the cracker. The associated
HDPE line is rated at 250 kta.

Coral Bay said the complex reached stable operation within six weeks of first feed.
