title: Bohai Sea Chemical reports record quarterly polyethylene output
---

Bohai Sea Chemical reported record quarterly output from its Tianjin HDPE line. The
company operates a 1,400 kta naphtha cracker at the site together with HDPE and
polypropylene lines.

The company said no capacity additions are planned at Tianjin before 2029.
