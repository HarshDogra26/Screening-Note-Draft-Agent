title: Java Timur adds swing capability at Gresik LLDPE line
---

Java Timur Petrochemical has modified its Gresik LLDPE line to allow production of
higher-density grades. The company said the modification does not change the line's
200 kta nameplate capacity but broadens the product slate.

The Gresik complex comprises a naphtha cracker, a propylene recovery unit, an LLDPE line
and a polypropylene line.
