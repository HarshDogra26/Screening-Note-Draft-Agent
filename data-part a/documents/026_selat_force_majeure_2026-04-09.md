title: Selat Kimia declares force majeure on ethylene deliveries
---

Selat Kimia Berhad has declared force majeure on ethylene deliveries from its Pengerang
complex with immediate effect.

The company said inspection during the current scheduled turnaround identified corrosion
in a quench tower internal that requires replacement, extending the outage beyond the
planned duration. Selat Kimia said it expects to lift the declaration once the unit
returns to service.

Downstream polyethylene and polypropylene lines at Pengerang are operating at reduced
rates on inventory. The company said it does not expect the work to affect nameplate
capacity once complete.
