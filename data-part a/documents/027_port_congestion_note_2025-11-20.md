title: Container and bulk congestion note - Southeast Asia
---

Berth congestion at several Southeast Asian container terminals lengthened waiting times
for polymer export cargoes through October and November, with delays of up to eleven days
reported at peak.

Resin shipments were among the affected categories. Regional polyethylene and
polypropylene offers firmed over the period as available prompt cargoes tightened.

Congestion eased from late November as additional berth capacity was brought back into
service following maintenance. This note covers logistics conditions only and contains no
price assessments or forward projections.
