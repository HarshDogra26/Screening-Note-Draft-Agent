title: Andaman completes Map Ta Phut cracker turnaround
---

Andaman Polymer Group has completed a scheduled turnaround at its Map Ta Phut steam
cracker. The unit was offline for 31 days. The company reported no change to capacity
and said all downstream units have resumed normal rates.
