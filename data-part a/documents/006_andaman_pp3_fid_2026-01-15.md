title: Andaman Polymer Group takes FID on third Map Ta Phut polypropylene line
---

Andaman Polymer Group has taken a final investment decision on a third polypropylene
line at its Map Ta Phut complex in Thailand. The line is designed for 300 kta and will
use gas-phase technology, consistent with the site's existing two lines.

The company said propylene will be supplied from the existing Map Ta Phut propylene
unit, and that no additional cracker capacity is required. First production was
targeted for 2028.

Andaman said the investment responds to demand growth in automotive and packaging
applications across Thailand and Vietnam.
