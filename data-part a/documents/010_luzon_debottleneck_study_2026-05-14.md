title: Luzon Resins begins debottlenecking study at Batangas
---

Luzon Resins Corporation has commissioned a study into debottlenecking options at its
Batangas cracker. The company said the study will assess whether ethylene output can be
increased without replacing the furnace section.

No target capacity has been set and the company said it will not comment on potential
figures until the study concludes, which it expects in the first half of 2027. The
company's propane dehydrogenation unit at the same site remains under construction.
