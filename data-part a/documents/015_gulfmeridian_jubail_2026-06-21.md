title: Gulf Meridian Chemicals advances second Jubail ethane cracker
---

Gulf Meridian Chemicals reported that its second ethane cracker at Jubail, Saudi Arabia,
is approximately 70 per cent complete, with startup expected in 2027. The unit is
designed for 1,300 kta of ethylene.

The company's first Jubail cracker, in operation since 2013, is rated at 1,500 kta.
Together the two units will give Gulf Meridian 2,800 kta of ethylene capacity at the
site.

Gulf Meridian Chemicals operates exclusively in the Middle East and has no assets in
Southeast Asia.
