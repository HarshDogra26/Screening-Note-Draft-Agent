title: Nusantara Olefins debottlenecks Cilegon polypropylene line
---

Nusantara Olefins has completed a debottlenecking of its Cilegon polypropylene line,
raising output capability. The company did not state a revised nameplate figure and said
it would confirm the new rating after a sustained performance test.

The company said propylene supply from the Cilegon propylene unit is sufficient to
support the higher rate without additional purchases.
