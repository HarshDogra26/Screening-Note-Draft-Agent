title: Mekong Chemical Industries signs naphtha supply agreement
---

Mekong Chemical Industries has signed a multi-year naphtha supply agreement covering
feedstock for its Vung Tau cracker. The company said the agreement secures a majority of
the unit's requirement from regional refineries.

Commercial terms were not disclosed.
