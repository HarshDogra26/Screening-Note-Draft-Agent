title: Naphtha supply and demand note - Asia Pacific
---

Regional naphtha balances tightened modestly over the first half of the year, driven by
refinery maintenance in Northeast Asia and steady petrochemical demand.

This note covers supply and trade flows only. It does not contain plant-level
petrochemical capacity data, and the Office does not publish producer-level capacity
figures.
