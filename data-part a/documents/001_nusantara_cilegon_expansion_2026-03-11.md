title: Nusantara Olefins completes Cilegon cracker expansion
---

Nusantara Olefins announced today that the expansion of Cilegon Cracker 1 has been
mechanically completed and the unit has returned to service. Following the expansion,
the company describes the unit as having an ethylene capacity of 1,200 kta.

The expansion replaced four cracking furnaces and upgraded the quench section. The
company said the project was executed during a planned 45-day turnaround and that
downstream polyethylene and polypropylene units at the Cilegon complex resumed normal
operation in the same week.

"This positions Cilegon as one of the most significant olefins complexes in the
region," said the company's Director of Operations. Nusantara Olefins did not disclose
the capital cost of the project.

The Cilegon complex also operates HDPE, LLDPE and polypropylene lines, together with a
propylene recovery unit. The company said it continues to evaluate a second cracker at
the site.
